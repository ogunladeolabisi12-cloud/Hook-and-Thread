About the Designer

An intimate reflection on modern fiber craft, mindful living, and the quiet ritual of crocheting and knitting of various designs of daily things people need and wear, One stitch at a time

Crocheting your Ideas into Reality

Hi, I'm Bisi. My journey with crocheting actually began 5 years ago when my friend in secondary school was crocheting as well and I was so suprised at what she was doing, just with a wool and crocheting pin.

At Hook and Thread, we create beautiful handmade crochet and knitted pieces designed to bring comfort, style, and personality to everyday life. From cozy headbands and head warmers to stylish sleeveless tops, scrunchies, and unique accessories, each piece adds a special touch to your wardrobe.

We also welcome custom orders, allowing you to choose your preferred sizes, yarn colors, and materials to suit your style. Our goal is to turn yarn into beautiful, meaningful creations that you can wear, cherish, and enjoy for years to come.
